# CLAUDE.md

Public teaching repo for Advanced Computational Methods in Physics (Jose Lado, Aalto). Before
writing or editing any prose here (lecture notes in `lecture_notes/src/`, notebook markdown in
`jupyter-notebooks/`, slide text), read the voice guide of the sibling course,
`../Advanced_Quantum_Materials_2027/VOICE.md`: the same lecturer, the same registers. The
notebook cells follow its section 4.4: the `##` titles, one-line intros and quiz bullets are
the lecturer's own and stay as they are, and the explanatory paragraph under each intro says
what the code does, gives the one idea needed to read the plot, and points at what to look at,
without answering the quiz bullets that follow it.
